## Offline review and physical correction

The revised monitor has synthetic positive tests for withdrawal, rapid retreat,
blocked effort, failed rest and conflicting commands; negative tests cover
straight passage, a normal standing transition and apparatus removal. It rejects
missing headings/gradients and stale samples, and latches hard stops. It is still
an incomplete investigation tool, not a welfare or consciousness detector.

An actual scene defect was found during mechanical qualification: FlyGym's
`Fly._add_floor_contacts` adds explicit contact pairs to unnamed arena geometry.
Those pairs override ordinary collision masks. Merely setting odor marker
contype/conaffinity to zero therefore did not make the markers non-colliding.
The new fixture uses visual sites instead of physical geoms; a regression test
ensures only the ground remains in the arena's geometry inventory.

Offline replay found source-marker contacts in 33 frames of the old stopped
neural run, beginning at .92s; the concentration maximum was at .93s. This is an
additional physical confound, not proof that contact caused the event, nor a
diagnosis of suffering. Earlier claims that these markers were non-physical
were incorrect. Original states, logs and stops remain preserved. The separate
archived-command body comparisons used flat terrain without odor markers and
are not affected by this bug.

The first mechanical v1 left-turn trial stopped at1.46s for blocked effort,
with marker contact beginning at1.205s. The other three mechanical trials are
retained too. Revised v2 tests use visual sites and the final prospective
inhibitory-output approach decoder. No neural model runs in these tests.
